## What Operators Do

### BOOTSTRAP Tier (Month 1-3)
Operators run 50+ nodes in a single region, providing capacity for:
- Development workloads (training, testing)
- Production workloads (stage 1: low-volume placement)
- Earn commissions on all workloads placed on their nodes

**SLA Target:** 95% uptime (baseline, achievable with standard infrastructure)

**Stake Requirement:** 100M uWork locked for 90 days minimum

**Earn Potential:** Baseline commission + 95% SLA bonus (if applicable)

### TRUSTED Tier (Month 3+)
After 90 days operation in BOOTSTRAP, operators graduate to:
- 100+ nodes across 2+ regions
- Higher SLA expectations (98% uptime)
- Higher stake (200M uWork)
- Increased commission structure

**Earn Potential:** Higher commission + SLA bonuses + reputation multipliers

### MASTER Tier (Year 2+)
Proven operators with 250+ nodes, 99.5% SLA, 500M uWork stake become:
- Federated operators (serve other operators' workloads)
- Replication authorities (manage cross-region consistency)
- Policy arbiters (govern operator disputes)

## Compensation & Economics

### Revenue Model

**Base Commission:** Per-workload fee
- Calculation: `(workload_cpu_hours + workload_memory_hours + workload_storage_hours) × base_price`
- Example: 1000 vCPU-hours/month @ $0.05/hour = $50/month base
- Paid monthly in arrears
- Minimum: $0 (if no workloads placed)

**SLA Bonus:** 95%+ uptime earns multiplier
- 95-97% uptime: +2% commission
- 97-99% uptime: +5% commission
- 99%+ uptime: +10% commission
- Calculated monthly, paid with base commission

**Reputation Multiplier:** Tier progression unlocks higher rates
- BOOTSTRAP: 1.0x base rate
- TRUSTED: 1.5x base rate
- MASTER: 2.0x base rate

**Example Economics (BOOTSTRAP Month 1):**
- 50 nodes, 100 vCPUs per node = 5,000 vCPU available
- Assume 30% utilization average = 1,500 vCPU-hours/day
- At $0.05/vCPU-hour + $0.02/GB-hour (100GB storage per workload):
  - Daily revenue: ~$75-100
  - Monthly revenue: ~$2,250-3,000
  - Less: Infrastructure costs (~$1,500-2,000/month for 50 nodes)
  - **Net monthly:** ~$250-1,500 (variable by workload mix)

**Plus SLA Bonus:** 95%+ uptime → 1.05x multiplier → +5-7% revenue
**Plus Tier Bonus:** Progression to TRUSTED → 1.5x multiplier → 50% revenue increase

**Breakeven Analysis:**
- Stake: 100M uWork (~$50,000-100,000)
- Infrastructure cost: ~$1,500-2,000/month
- Time to ROI: 18-24 months (conservative estimate with moderate utilization)

## FAQ for Prospective Operators

**Q: What if I can't commit 100M uWork?**  
A: BOOTSTRAP tier requires 100M minimum. We have a waiting list for Phase 2 operators in month 2+ when TRUSTED tier opens (200M requirement allows operator pooling).

**Q: What's the failure rate for nodes?**  
A: In Phase 6 testing, <0.1% error rate on workload placement. Node hardware failures expected to be <5%/year (same as industry standard).

**Q: Can I use cloud VMs instead of physical hardware?**  
A: Yes. VMs are acceptable if they meet 95% uptime SLA and have dedicated network bandwidth (1-2 Gbps minimum).

**Q: What if my SLA drops below 95%?**  
A: You lose SLA bonuses (2-10% revenue reduction) but remain operational. After 30 consecutive days <95%, Decentralized.Host may recommend you pause new workload acceptance and focus on remediation.

**Q: How long until TRUSTED tier?**  
A: 90 days of sustained 95%+ SLA operation, plus node expansion to 100+. Most operators target month 3-4 progression.

**Q: What's the exit strategy?**  
A: You can exit anytime after 90-day minimum stake lock. Workloads migrate off your nodes over 7 days (workload owners have time to rebalance). Stake released within 14 days of exit notice.

**Q: Can I use my own pricing/policies?**  
A: No. Decentralized.Host sets base pricing globally for fairness. Your local policy (per-node admission control) can add restrictions but not override pricing.
