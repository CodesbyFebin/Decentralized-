# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of:
- Age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, expression, experience level
- Nationality, personal appearance, political beliefs, race, religion, sexual identity, orientation
- Socioeconomic status, technical background

This applies to all spaces: code repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and community events.

## Our Standards

### Positive Behaviors

- **Be respectful.** Treat others with kindness and professional courtesy.
- **Listen actively.** Consider different perspectives before responding.
- **Give constructive feedback.** Focus on ideas and code, not people. Be specific and actionable.
- **Acknowledge mistakes.** Apologize sincerely and work to do better.
- **Help others learn.** Share knowledge generously. Mentor newcomers.
- **Assume good intent.** Most people are doing their best.

### Unacceptable Behaviors

- Harassment, discrimination, or hostile comments based on protected characteristics
- Sexual harassment, unwanted romantic advances, or inappropriate physical contact
- Trolling, insulting comments, personal attacks, or demeaning language
- Threats, intimidation, or abusive behavior
- Doxxing or sharing private information without consent
- Spam or advertising (unless solicited)
- Plagiarism or claiming credit for others' work

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- The GitHub repository (issues, PRs, discussions)
- Code comments and commit messages
- Project communication channels
- Community events and meetups
- Any space where people represent the Decentralized.Host project

## Enforcement

### Reporting Violations

If you witness or experience a violation, please report it:

1. **In private:** Email [conduct@decentralized-host.dev](mailto:codesbyfebin@gmail.com?subject=Code%20of%20Conduct%20Violation)
2. **Include:**
   - Description of the violation
   - Who was involved (if comfortable sharing)
   - When and where it happened
   - Any relevant links or screenshots
   - Your contact information (optional)

3. **Confidentiality:** Reports are treated confidentially. We won't share details without your consent.

### Response & Resolution

1. **Acknowledgment:** We'll respond within 48 hours
2. **Investigation:** We'll gather facts and context
3. **Resolution:** We'll take appropriate action, which may include:
   - Private conversation with the person
   - Public warning or apology
   - Temporary or permanent ban from the project
4. **Transparency:** We'll communicate outcomes as appropriate

## Exceptions & Appeals

- **Context matters.** We consider context, intent, and impact when investigating.
- **Mistakes happen.** We believe in redemption. People can learn and change.
- **Appeals:** If you disagree with an enforcement decision, you can appeal within 30 days.

## Examples

### ✅ Good Interactions

**Code review:**
> "I think we could simplify this loop using a closure. What do you think about...?"

**Disagreement:**
> "I see your point, but I'm concerned about performance. Here's a benchmark showing... Can we discuss alternatives?"

**Helping newcomers:**
> "Great first PR! The logic looks good. One thing: we typically use `context.Context` for cancellation. Here's an example..."

### ❌ Unacceptable Interactions

**Dismissive:**
> "Only an idiot would write code like this."

**Harassing:**
> "People like you shouldn't be programming."

**Gatekeeping:**
> "You're not a 'real' engineer if you haven't used [obscure framework]."
