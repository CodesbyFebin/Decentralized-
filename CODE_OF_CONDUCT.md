# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful environment for everyone in the Decentralized.Host community. We value the participation of every individual and want to ensure that all people, regardless of background or identity, feel welcomed and supported.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that creates a positive environment include:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Harassing or discriminatory language or conduct
- Personal attacks or insults
- Trolling, intimidating, or threatening behavior
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Other conduct that is unprofessional or inappropriate

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces managed by Decentralized.Host, including:
- GitHub repositories and discussions
- Pull request reviews and comments
- Issue tracking and discussions
- Code contributions
- Communication channels (Discord, Slack, email, etc.)

It also applies to representatives of the project in public spaces when acting in an official capacity.

## Enforcement

### Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it by:
- Sending an email to codesbyfebin@gmail.com with details
- Opening a private security advisory on GitHub
- Contacting a project maintainer directly

Include:
- A description of the incident
- When and where it occurred
- People involved (if known)
- Any relevant links or evidence

All reports will be reviewed confidentially.

### Handling Reports

1. **Investigation**: Maintainers will investigate the report promptly
2. **Response**: We will communicate with the involved parties
3. **Resolution**: Appropriate action will be taken to address the violation
4. **Appeal**: Individuals can appeal decisions through a defined process

## Consequences

Violations may result in:
- A private warning and discussion
- Public or private written apology requirement
- Temporary or permanent ban from contributing
- Removal from project roles or leadership

The severity and context of the violation will determine the response.
