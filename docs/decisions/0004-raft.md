# 0004 — Raft for the control plane; Postgres optional

**Status:** accepted

Desired state, the audit ledger and committed evidence live in a replicated
Raft log (hashicorp/raft with raft-boltdb, over mutual TLS with root-issued
member certificates). Postgres is an optional, asynchronous *mirror* for
queries, never the source of truth.

**Why.** Blueprint M5 requires no split-brain writes, a durable log,
failover and restore without an external database. A 3- or 5-member Raft
group gives that with a single binary.

**Consequences.**
- The FSM is deterministic, with time carried in commands.
- Bundles carry the log index, so hosts reject rollbacks.
- Backups are signed exports of FSM state, and restores continue the index
  space.
- The console reports durability as `RAFT COMMITTED`, `DB COMMITTED` or
  `JOURNAL ONLY`, whichever is true.
